/cybersecurity

Practical, ethical, hands-on.

Cybersecurity is the part of IT that pulled me in the hardest — the mix of curiosity and discipline it takes to understand how systems fail, and how to make them fail safely, on purpose, in a lab.

Everything here is educational: CTF challenges, sandboxed environments, and ethical-hacking practice. No live targets, no real infrastructure — just structured ways to learn how attacks and defenses actually work.
